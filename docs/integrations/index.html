Mach Five Magnet and HubSpot

Mach Five Magnet and Salesforce

Every captured lead becomes a Salesforce lead automatically, with the magnet conversation as a note on the record.

Mach Five Magnet and Zoho CRM

Every captured lead lands in the Zoho CRM Leads module automatically, with the magnet conversation as a note.

Mach Five Magnet and Microsoft Bookings

Visitors book through your Microsoft Bookings page from a magnet. Each booking is added to the lead, with the Teams meeting link included.

Mach Five Magnet and HighLevel

Every captured lead becomes a HighLevel contact automatically, with the conversation as a note and a tag your workflows can use.

Mach Five Magnet and Cal.com

Visitors book your Cal.com events from a magnet. Each booking is added to the lead. Works on every Cal.com plan, including free.

Mach Five Magnet and CallRail

Every tracked phone call becomes a lead automatically, complete with recording and source.

Mach Five Magnet and Webhook

Send every captured and qualified lead to your own endpoint as signed JSON.
