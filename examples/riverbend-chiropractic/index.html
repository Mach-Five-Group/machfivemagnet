About the clinic

A clinic that treats you, not a template

Dr. Dana Whitfield opened Riverbend in 2019 after a decade in sports rehab, with one rule: every plan has an end date. We combine chiropractic adjustment with soft-tissue work and simple take-home exercises, and we tell you honestly when you no longer need us.
